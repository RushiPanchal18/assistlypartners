FAQ

Pricing Questions

What does support actually cost?

Pricing starts from approximately $5/hour and scales with specialization and hours. Specialist work — bookkeeping, automation buildouts, transaction coordination — is priced above general administrative support.

Is there a long-term contract?

Engagements are monthly. Scale hours up in a busy season and back down after, without a hiring or severance cycle either way.

What is not included?

We provide administrative and operational support. We do not provide legal, tax, or accounting advice, and we work alongside your CPA, attorney, or broker rather than replacing them.

How are hours tracked?

You get regular reporting on what was handled and where the time went, so the retainer stays connected to visible output.
